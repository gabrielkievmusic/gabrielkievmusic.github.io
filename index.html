About Me

My name is Gabriel Kiev, I am a brazilian physicist, musician and sound designer, currently living in Portugal. As an artist, I am mostly passionate about sound synthesis and experimental music production. My work is quite ecclectic, ranging from generative ambient soundscapes to progressive rock compositions, film scoring and sound design, and beyond.

My education is in Physics Engineering at the University of Porto, and I am now working on my Master's degree with a focus on the intersection of physics and music technology. If you wish to talk to me, you can send me an e-mail straight through the Mail app.

This website is both a digital portfolio and a way for me to learn web development as a hobby. Around here, most programs are a way for me to showcase a bit of sound design or music programming while having some fun. I hope you'll have fun with them as well! On the Radio, you can listen to a more varied selection of songs, including some of my unreleased tracks and a few songs by my friends and fellow artists, among which I include my own prog-rock/post-punk/no-wave band ANOMNIA, which you should definitely check out!

Beyond my personal projects, I have also worked as:

  • Sound Designer for a promotional video

  • Sound Director, Recordist, Mixer, Designer, Foley Artist and Soundtrack Composer for upcoming short film "Linha de Espera"

Introduction

Welcome to my Musical Operating System.

The MOS is a collection of musical experiments and audiovisual coding projects. By navigating the desktop you will see a few diferent programs. They range from visual interpretations of my musical work to interactable soundscapes and even some experimental digital synthesizers.

This guide is designed to provide information on all the programs currently present. Most of them have a direct link to their corresponding guide section via the [i] button on the top right of their windows.

Please note that the Musical Operating System is in constant development, with many updates being released frequently and many more to come. Since I am not deeply trained in web development, you may experience bugs. If that does happen, you are encouraged to send a bug report via the Mail app.

Explore around, create some sounds and leave them playing on the background or use them for your own projects!
