About Me

My name is Gabriel Kiev, I am a brazilian physicist, musician and sound designer, currently living in Portugal. As an artist, I am mostly passionate about sound synthesis and experimental music production. My work is quite ecclectic, ranging from generative ambient soundscapes to progressive rock compositions, film scoring and sound design, and beyond.

My formation is in Physics Engineering at the University of Porto, and I am now working on my Master's degree with a focus on the intersection of physics and music technology. This website is both a digital portfolio and a way for me to learn web development as a hobby, so feel free to explore and send me a message via the Mail app.

Around here, each program is a way to showcase my sound design in a way that's more interesting than posting a boring list of links. On the Radio, you can listen to a more varied selection of songs, including some of my unreleased tracks and a few songs by my friends and fellow artists, among which I include my own prog-rock/post-punk/no-wave band ANOMNIA.
